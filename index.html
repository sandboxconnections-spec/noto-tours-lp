I — Mindfulness

Zen & Sea

An hour of silence at a temple of six centuries

Learn zazen at a former head temple, eat in silence, work with your hands, and sit by the sea. End the day by lighting a candle you chose yourself.

Sojiji Soin was the head temple of the Soto school for almost 600 years. You learn the form of zazen and sit for thirty minutes. In the afternoon you carve a pattern into lacquer chopsticks at a craftsman’s shop, then pick a hand-made Japanese candle on Ipponsugi Street to light after dinner.

II — Gourmet

Sushi and Its Origins

Follow the soy sauce all the way to the counter

By day, learn the soy sauce and the fish, then shape sushi yourself and feel how hard it is. At night, a master’s counter gives you the answer.

The soy sauce comes from Torii Shoyu, a hundred-year-old brewery in a registered cultural property on Ipponsugi Street. In the afternoon, a local sushi chef teaches your group to shape a few pieces — just a few, to save room for dinner. Dinner is at Taisho Sushi near the station, where you sit with the regulars and eat what they eat.

III — Activity

Paddle the Bay

On water so still it barely moves

Next season · from May 2027

Paddle the calm water off Notojima Island in the morning, work in a rice field in the afternoon, and end with dinner upstairs at an old shop in town.

Nanao Bay is enclosed by Notojima Island, so the water is usually flat enough for first-timers. The paddle is with a local outdoor guide and only your group on the water. In season, the afternoon is rice planting (May) or harvest (September). This year’s season has ended. Join the waitlist and we will write to you first when dates for May 2027 open.

Before you book

ご予約の前に

Is it all right to visit Noto now?

Yes. The 2024 earthquake hit the peninsula hard, and Nanao is still rebuilding. The places on these days are open, and we confirm every stop before we confirm your date. Coming here, eating here and paying local makers is one of the most direct ways to help.

Who will be with you

A small team that lives in Nanao. We run SandBoxConnections, a local company working with the town’s shops, makers and community groups. One of us stays with you the whole day and speaks English.

What your day pays for

  • Only your group, all day, with a local host
  • Time with the people themselves — the brewer, the chef, the outdoor guide — arranged for you
  • Every workshop, meal and entry on the day

How booking works

  1. Send a request with your day, two dates, and the size of your group.
  2. We check with every stop and reply by email within 48 hours.
  3. You receive a secure card payment link. Your day is held once it is paid.

If plans change

Free cancellation up to 14 days before your day. 50% is refunded from 13 to 4 days before; within 3 days, no refund. If weather or one of our hosts makes the day impossible, we offer another date or a full refund.

Prices include consumption tax (10%). Lodging and travel to Nanao Station are not included.

Getting to Nanao

Every day starts and ends at Nanao Station, in the middle of the Noto Peninsula, on the JR Nanao Line from Kanazawa.

The days end in the evening, after the last train back to Kanazawa has left. Plan to stay the night in Nanao or at Wakura Onsen nearby.
